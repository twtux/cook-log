# COOK PLAN — 9.6 lb bone-in Pork Butt → Pork Steaks + Pulled Pork
**Rig:** Spider Huntsman + Venom · one grill, two separate cooks · Heath Riles rub

## STANDING RULES (baked in)
- 🧂 ONE salt step only. The **Heath Riles rub = the salt** — do NOT salt/brine on top.
- 🪵 **Wood chunk every cook** (hickory or apple for pork). Smarty Pants reminds at fire-lay.
- 🔥 **Chimney start → full basket of coals (Minion)** so the fire can't die mid-cook (tonight's lesson).

## SATURDAY — break it down + pulled pork
1. **Cut:** firm the butt in the freezer 30–45 min (cleaner slices).
   - Off the meatier end, cut **4–6 pork steaks, 1 to 1¼" thick** (work around the blade bone).
   - Leave a **~4–5 lb bone-in chunk whole** for pulled pork.
2. **Steaks:** rub with **Heath Riles (+ garlic powder or their Garlic Butter blend)** → fridge till Sunday. (Rub = salt, no more.)
3. **Pulled pork (all-day Huntsman cook):**
   - Rub the chunk (Heath Riles / pork rub = its salt).
   - Fire: chimney start → full basket, **wood chunk on**, Venom **250–275°**.
   - **Wrap in foil/paper at ~165°** (through the stall) → cook to **203–205°** internal.
   - **Rest 30+ min** (holds wrapped in a cooler for hours) → pull. ~1–1.5 hr/lb, so morning start.

## SUNDAY (or later) — pork steaks (St. Louis style)
- Already rubbed. **Sear both sides over the coals (~350°)** for color/bark.
- **Braise:** foil pan + BBQ sauce + splash of beer/stock, **covered, ~300°** until **fork-tender (~200–205°)**, about 1–1.5 hr.
- Sauce and serve. (The braise is what makes them melt.)
