# Cook Log — First Grind-Your-Own Burger Blend 🍔🥩

**Date:** Sat 2026-08-15 (morning grind/prep; cook to follow)
**Cook:** Ground my **own** burger blend for the first time — the classic steakhouse trinity.
**Rig (grind):** MEAT! electric grinder. Cook surface TBD (flat-top / cast iron).
**Goal:** one blend to serve both **loose-ball smash burgers** and **half-pound pub burgers**, plus a batch to freeze.

## The blend (the "LaFrieda trinity")
- **Beef Chuck** (USDA Choice) — ~3.1 lb, $23.08 — lean, beefy base
- **Beef Short Rib** (USDA Choice Angus) — ~3.0 lb, $32.91 — deep, rich, fatty flavor
- **Brisket trimmings** (from the day before) — extra fat + brisket funk
- Target fat: **~20–25%**. Suggested ratio going forward: **~50% chuck / 30% short rib / 20% brisket trim.**

## What happened / lessons
- **Grinder parts were frozen for days** (good) — but on the **second grind pass the meat turned gooey/smeary.**
  - **Cause:** the *meat itself* warmed up during the first pass (friction + handling), not the parts. Fat starts to smear/emulsify → pasty texture.
  - **Fix (Tom's own instinct, correct):** grind once → **spread on a tray and chill/near-freeze the ground meat 20–30 min** → grind again → **chill again before pressing patties.**
  - **Why double-grind here:** the first (coarse) plate leaves the grind **too big/chunky** for burgers, so a second pass is needed to bring it down to burger size. **Standing rule: chill the meat back down between grind #1 and grind #2**, and chill again before pressing patties.
  - **One-pass alternative for next time:** get a **medium plate (~4.5mm / ~3/16")** and do a **single pass** — burger-sized in one go, no smear, looser texture. Only reach for the double-grind routine when the coarse plate is all you've got.
- **Result of the grind:** great fat distribution — even white flecks through the red. Coarse and craggy = ideal for lacy smash edges + structured pub patties.

## Tools
- **Weber dual burger press** — half-pound side + quarter-pound side.
- **Rule locked in:** *Freeze = pressed patties. Eat-today smash = loose balls.* (A frozen ball won't smash flat.)

## Handling rules (locked in)
- Keep meat **cold** the whole time; pull small amounts, portion, rest goes back in the fridge.
- **No salt in the grind** — salt only the outside right before it hits heat (salt in the mix = bouncy/sausagey).
- **Don't remix/mush** the ground pile — every handling tightens the texture.
- **Smash:** loose 2 oz balls, smash hard once on a screaming surface, salt after, flip when edges are brown/crispy (~90s/side).
- **Half-pounders:** press to shape (don't compact), thumb-dimple center, cook to a true **medium (~135°F+)** for food safety on a grind.

## Freezing the extras
1. Press into patties, **parchment square between each**.
2. **Flash-freeze flat** on a tray 1–2 hrs until firm (not touching).
3. Stack into a **freezer bag / vacuum seal**, press ALL air out. Label + date. ~3 months quality.
4. **Cook from frozen:** thin patties go straight on the hot griddle; half-pounders thaw overnight in the fridge first.

## Grinder care (MEAT!)
- Push the last bit through with a slice of **white bread** to clear the auger/plate.
- **Freeze plate/blade/auger 20 min** before grinding next time.
- Hand-wash + **dry immediately** (carbon-steel plate surface-rusts if air-dried wet); wipe with food-safe oil before storage.
