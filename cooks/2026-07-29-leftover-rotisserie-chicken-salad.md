# Cook Log — Leftover Rotisserie Chicken Salad 🥗🐔

**Logged:** Wed 2026-07-29 (recovered from memory — made recently from the 7/24 chicken)
**Cook:** Chicken salad built from **leftover Huntsman rotisserie chicken** (see `2026-07-24-huntsman-rotisserie-chicken.md`).
**No cooking** — assembly from leftovers.

## Key move
- **De-skin the chicken.** The rotisserie bird was a little **salty**, so pulling the skin off cut the salt down. (Do this — it's what made it work.)

## Ingredients (Tom's amounts)
- **3 cups** chopped leftover rotisserie chicken (skin removed)
- **1 cup** **Kewpie** mayonnaise
- **1/4 cup** sour cream
- **3 celery sticks**, chopped
- **Red grapes** (halved/whole to taste)
- **Red onion**, minced _(added — per Tom's recollection)_
- **Lemon** juice _(added)_
- **Dijon mustard** _(added)_

## Method
1. **De-skin** the leftover chicken, then chop (~3 cups).
2. Mix **Kewpie mayo + sour cream** for the dressing, with **lemon juice + Dijon** worked in.
3. Fold in chicken, **chopped celery**, **minced red onion**, and **red grapes**.
4. Chill / serve.

## Notes / next time
- **1 cup mayo + 1/4 cup sour cream to 3 cups chicken** is on the **rich/creamy** side — start with less and add to taste if you want it lighter.
- Skin-off is the salt fix — remember it, especially with a salty rotisserie bird.
- Kewpie carries here too (same as the cheesesteak). Tom's house mayo.

## Upgrade version (reference — NOT yet made, try next time) 🧑‍🍳
_A dressed-up take to try. Differs from Tom's actual cook above — added onion, lemon, Dijon, dill; shredded not diced; yogurt option. Smart salt trick: keep the dressing under-seasoned since the chicken brings the salt._

**Serves 4**
- 500 g shredded rotisserie chicken, skin discarded
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1/3 cup plain Greek yogurt **or** sour cream
- 2 celery stalks, diced small
- 1/2 small red onion, minced
- juice of 1 lemon
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1/2 tsp freshly cracked black pepper
- 2 Tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- 1 cup red grapes, halved (optional)

**Steps**
1. **Shred** the chicken off the bone (more surface area than cubes → dressing spreads the salt evenly, no salty hot spots). Discard skin — that's where the seasoning concentrates.
2. **Whisk the dressing:** mayo, yogurt/sour cream, lemon juice, Dijon, black pepper. It should taste slightly *under*-seasoned and a little tart — correct, the chicken brings the salt.
3. **Fold in** chicken, celery, red onion, dill, grapes until coated.
4. **Chill ≥30 min** so the salt equalizes. Taste again — if still sharp, add a spoon more yogurt/mayo.
