# Cook Log — Leftover-Ribeye Philly Cheesesteak 🥩🧀

**Date:** Mon 2026-07-27 (dinner, ~5:50 PM)
**Cook:** Philly cheesesteak built from **leftover cooked ribeye** — quick stovetop, not BBQ.
**Rig:** Skillet on the stovetop, medium heat.
**Goal:** finely **shredded** beef texture (Tom dislikes the big chunks he normally gets) + melty, loaded.

## Ingredients (what Tom actually used)
- Leftover cooked **ribeye** (rock-chopped shredded)
- **Onion** (pre-cut), **green pepper**, **mushrooms** (in the finished photo), **butter**
- **Beef broth** (in the meat pan)
- **White cheese** (melted on top — see photo)
- **Publix hoagie buns** (pack of 2), **Kewpie mayonnaise** (Tom's go-to — MSG + rice-vinegar richness) — **NOT toasted**
- **Green pepper is optional** — Tom often skips it (onions + mushrooms carry the veg)

## Method — TWO PANS (what he actually did)
1. **Shred the beef by hand.** Slice the cooked ribeye **thin, ACROSS the grain** (the one cut that matters — shortens the fibers so it falls apart instead of chunking), then **rock-chop** with a chef's knife → shredded, no big chunks. (Skipped the food processor/coffee-grinder ideas.)
2. **Pan 1 — the veg.** Butter, **sautéed onions**, then added **green peppers**, then **more onions on top of the peppers**, kept cooking. (Mushrooms in the mix per the photo.)
3. **Pan 2 — the meat (at the same time).** Second pan: **butter + beef broth**, heated it up, then **put the shredded meat in** to warm through in the broth.
4. **Buns.** **Publix hoagie buns** (2-pack), spread with **Kewpie mayonnaise** — **did NOT toast them, didn't do anything to them.**
5. **Build.** Meat + veg onto the mayo buns, white cheese melted over the top.

## VERDICT (Tom)
- **"That was fantastic."** 🙌 (Also called it "actually pretty yummy" mid-eat.) First go, **shredded texture nailed it** — no big chunks. The **two-pan approach worked great**, and **mayo + untoasted Publix buns needed zero fuss.** Better than most takeout. (Photo on file.)

## Notes / next time
- **Two pans = the move here** — veg in one, meat warming in butter+broth in the other. No crowding, everything ready together.
- **The shred trick:** against-the-grain first cut + rock-chop. Repeat it.
- **Mayo on untoasted Publix buns worked** — simple, tasty, no extra step. (Toast + butter is optional if you want crunch, but not needed.)
- Warm the beef fast in the broth (it's leftover/pre-cooked) — don't over-simmer.
- **Cut it COLD.** Leftover ribeye dices/shreds far cleaner straight from the fridge (firm = clean cuts; warm = tears/squishes). Cut cold, then revive in the warm butter+broth. Knife rock-chop is the best tool — skip the box grater/food processor. Diced is Tom's actual default (great chew); shred only if you want the wispy melt-together texture.
