# Cook Log — Leftover Brisket BBQ Pizza 🍕🔥 (personal pan, cast iron)

**Date:** Mon 2026-08-17 · Lake Mary, FL
**Type:** Leftover cook — repurposing Friday's brisket
**Source brisket:** [`2026-08-14-brisket-prime-packer-huntsman.md`](2026-08-14-brisket-prime-packer-huntsman.md) (USDA Prime packer, Huntsman, no wrap)
**Vessel:** Cast iron (personal pan size)

## Build (bottom → top)
| Layer | What |
|---|---|
| Dough | Pre-made **personal-pan pizza dough** (gifted — "Seminole County"; didn't make it myself) |
| Base sauce | **Sweet Baby Ray's** regular BBQ sauce |
| Accent | A little **ranch** (Hidden Valley) — *not much*, just a hidden touch |
| Protein | **Leftover smoked brisket** (Fri 8/14 cook) |
| Veg | **Red onions** |
| Cheese | **Hand-shredded mozzarella** |
| Pickles | **Thin-sliced sandwich (dill) pickles — patted SUPER dry, added AFTER the bake (on the rest)** |

Clean build. No buffalo (the orange was the SBR + ranch). **Key technique:** the pickles went on
**post-bake, during the rest** (and patted bone-dry first) — so they stay cool, crisp, and tangy
instead of cooking down soft, and they never steam the crust. That's why the bottom stayed shatter-crisp.

**Finish:** post-bake, topped with a **ranch zigzag drizzle + a splash of SBR** across the slice —
BBQ-joint presentation, and an extra sauce hit up front.

## Result
- Cast iron delivered a **leopard-spotted, crispy edge** and a crust with real structure.
- Cooled on a **wire rack** (kept the bottom crisp — no soggy steam-out).
- Verdict: 🔥. Friday brisket's second life as pizza royalty. The few-day rest let the bark mellow into the whole thing.
