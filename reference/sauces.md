# Sauce Reference 🍯🔥

Base recipes you can run as-is or tweak per cook. Log the exact recipe + tweaks in that cook's **Seasoning** field so you can run winners back.

**Rule of thumb:** build a sauce in this order so it balances — sweet (sugar/molasses), tang (vinegar), heat (pepper/chili), savory/depth (Worcestershire, mustard, garlic), then thin to the texture you want with broth or water.

---

## Kansas City (the all-purpose sweet-sticky) — start here
The crowd-pleaser. Great on ribs, pulled pork, chicken, burnt ends.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Ketchup | 1½ cups |
| Dark brown sugar | ⅓ cup |
| Molasses (unsulphured) | 2 tbsp |
| Apple cider vinegar | ¼ cup |
| Worcestershire sauce | 2 tbsp |
| Yellow mustard | 1 tbsp |
| Smoked paprika | 2 tsp |
| Garlic powder | 1 tsp |
| Onion powder | 1 tsp |
| Black pepper | 1 tsp |
| Cayenne | ¼ tsp (more for heat) |

**Method:** whisk everything cold in a saucepan, bring to a bare simmer, cook 15–20 min stirring so it doesn't scorch. It thickens as it cools. Thin with a splash of water if too thick.

---

## Carolina Vinegar (thin, tangy — the pulled-pork finisher)
Not a glaze — a dressing you toss pulled pork in. Cuts the fat, wakes everything up.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Apple cider vinegar | 1 cup |
| Water | ½ cup |
| Dark brown sugar | 2 tbsp |
| Red pepper flakes | 1 tbsp |
| Louisiana-style hot sauce | 1 tbsp |
| Kosher salt | 1 tsp |
| Black pepper | 1 tsp |

**Method:** shake in a jar, let sit 1 hr+ so the flakes bloom. No cooking. Sprinkle over pork right before serving.

## Alabama White (put this on your rotisserie chicken)
Made for smoked chicken and turkey. Unexpected and people love it.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Mayonnaise (Duke's) | 1 cup |
| Apple cider vinegar | ¼ cup |
| Prepared horseradish | 1 tbsp |
| Dijon mustard | 1 tbsp |
| Lemon juice | 1 tbsp |
| Black pepper | 1 tsp (coarse) |
| Garlic powder | ½ tsp |
| Cayenne | ¼ tsp |

**Method:** whisk, refrigerate 30 min. Drizzle on chicken at the table, or dunk. Keep it off direct high heat (mayo breaks).

---

## Texas Mop (for brisket — barely a sauce)
Let the beef talk. This is a thin mop / au jus, not a thick glaze.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Beef broth (low-sodium) | 1 cup |
| Reserved brisket drippings | ¼ cup |
| Apple cider vinegar | 2 tbsp |
| Worcestershire sauce | 1 tbsp |
| Black pepper (coarse) | 1 tsp |
| Kosher salt | ½ tsp |

**Method:** warm, spoon over sliced brisket. That's it.

---

## Notes
- **Sugar burns.** Any sauce with brown sugar/molasses goes on in the **last 20–30 min** over indirect heat, or at the table — not early over a hot fire.
- **Make ahead:** all of these keep 1–2 weeks in a sealed jar in the fridge; flavors improve overnight.
