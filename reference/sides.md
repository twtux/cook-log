# Sides Reference 🌽🔥

Smoker sides that ride alongside the meat. Where it helps, set the pan **under the meat** so drippings fall in.

---

## Smoky BBQ Green Beans
The kind that sit in the smoker soaking up bacon fat and drippings.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Fresh green beans, trimmed | 2 lb |
| Thick-cut bacon, chopped | 6 slices |
| Yellow onion, diced | ½ |
| Garlic, minced | 3 cloves |
| Low-sodium chicken broth | 1 cup |
| Dark brown sugar | 2 tbsp |
| Apple cider vinegar | 1 tbsp |
| Your BBQ rub | 1 tbsp |
| BBQ sauce (optional) | splash |
| Kosher salt + black pepper | to taste |

**Method**
1. In a cast-iron or foil pan, render the **thick-cut bacon** until it's giving up fat but not fully crisp. Add **yellow onion + garlic**, cook till soft.
2. Add **beans, broth, brown sugar, vinegar, rub.** Stir.
3. Into the smoker at **250–275°F, uncovered, ~1.5–2 hrs**, stirring once or twice. Add broth if it dries out.
4. Last 20 min, splash of BBQ sauce if you want them sticky-sweet.

**Pro move:** cook them **under the meat** so the drippings fall right in.

## Smoked Mac & Cheese
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Elbow macaroni | 1 lb |
| Butter | 4 tbsp |
| Flour | ¼ cup |
| Whole milk | 3 cups |
| Sharp cheddar, shredded | 3 cups |
| Gruyère (or Monterey Jack), shredded | 1 cup |
| Cream cheese | 4 oz |
| Your BBQ rub | 1 tsp |
| Kosher salt + black pepper | to taste |

**Method:** boil macaroni 2 min shy of done. Make a roux (butter + flour, 1 min), whisk in milk till it thickens, kill heat, stir in cheeses + cream cheese + rub. Fold in macaroni, pour into a cast-iron/foil pan, top with a little extra cheddar. Smoke at **275°F ~1 hr** until bubbly with a smoky top. Shred your own cheese — bagged cheese has anti-caking starch that makes it grainy.

## Smoked Baked Beans (great under pork or brisket)
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Canned baked beans (Bush's Original) | 2 × 28 oz |
| Thick-cut bacon, chopped | 6 slices |
| Yellow onion, diced | 1 |
| Green bell pepper, diced | 1 |
| Dark brown sugar | ½ cup |
| Yellow mustard | 2 tbsp |
| Apple cider vinegar | 2 tbsp |
| BBQ sauce | ½ cup |
| Chopped brisket/pork trimmings (optional) | 1 cup |

**Method:** render bacon, soften onion + green pepper in the fat, stir in everything else in a cast-iron pan. Smoke **uncovered at 275°F, 2–2.5 hrs** until thick and caramelized on top. Best pan to park **under a brisket or pork butt** — the drippings make it.

## Quick Coleslaw (the acid that cuts the fat)
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Bagged coleslaw mix | 14 oz |
| Mayonnaise (Duke's) | ½ cup |
| Apple cider vinegar | 2 tbsp |
| Dijon mustard | 1 tbsp |
| Sugar | 1 tbsp |
| Celery seed | ½ tsp |
| Kosher salt + black pepper | to taste |

**Method:** whisk the dressing, toss with the mix, chill 1 hr. This is the fresh, sharp bite that balances a rich plate of smoked meat — don't skip it.

---

## Notes
- **Cast iron or foil pans** on the smoker — nothing you care about scrubbing.
- **Under the meat** = free flavor for beans and greens.
- Sides that take smoke well: beans, greens, mac, corn, potatoes. Delicate stuff (slaw) stays cold.
