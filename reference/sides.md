# Sides Reference 🌽🔥

Smoker sides that ride alongside the meat. Where it helps, set the pan **under the meat** so drippings fall in.

---

## Smoky BBQ Green Beans
The kind that sit in the smoker soaking up bacon fat and drippings.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Fresh green beans, trimmed | 2 lb |
| Thick-cut bacon, chopped | 6 slices |
| Yellow onion, diced | ½ |
| Garlic, minced | 3 cloves |
| Low-sodium chicken broth | 1 cup |
| Dark brown sugar | 2 tbsp |
| Apple cider vinegar | 1 tbsp |
| Your BBQ rub | 1 tbsp |
| BBQ sauce (optional) | splash |
| Kosher salt + black pepper | to taste |

**Method**
1. In a cast-iron or foil pan, render the **thick-cut bacon** until it's giving up fat but not fully crisp. Add **yellow onion + garlic**, cook till soft.
2. Add **beans, broth, brown sugar, vinegar, rub.** Stir.
3. Into the smoker at **250–275°F, uncovered, ~1.5–2 hrs**, stirring once or twice. Add broth if it dries out.
4. Last 20 min, splash of BBQ sauce if you want them sticky-sweet.

**Pro move:** cook them **under the meat** so the drippings fall right in.

## Smoked Mac & Cheese
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Elbow macaroni | 1 lb |
| Butter | 4 tbsp |
| Flour | ¼ cup |
| Whole milk | 3 cups |
| Sharp cheddar, shredded | 3 cups |
| Gruyère (or Monterey Jack), shredded | 1 cup |
| Cream cheese | 4 oz |
| Your BBQ rub | 1 tsp |
| Kosher salt + black pepper | to taste |

**Method:** boil macaroni 2 min shy of done. Make a roux (butter + flour, 1 min), whisk in milk till it thickens, kill heat, stir in cheeses + cream cheese + rub. Fold in macaroni, pour into a cast-iron/foil pan, top with a little extra cheddar. Smoke at **275°F ~1 hr** until bubbly with a smoky top. Shred your own cheese — bagged cheese has anti-caking starch that makes it grainy.

## Smoked Baked Beans (great under pork or brisket)
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Canned baked beans (Bush's Original) | 2 × 28 oz |
| Thick-cut bacon, chopped | 6 slices |
| Yellow onion, diced | 1 |
| Green bell pepper, diced | 1 |
| Dark brown sugar | ½ cup |
| Yellow mustard | 2 tbsp |
| Apple cider vinegar | 2 tbsp |
| BBQ sauce | ½ cup |
| Chopped brisket/pork trimmings (optional) | 1 cup |

**Method:** render bacon, soften onion + green pepper in the fat, stir in everything else in a cast-iron pan. Smoke **uncovered at 275°F, 2–2.5 hrs** until thick and caramelized on top. Best pan to park **under a brisket or pork butt** — the drippings make it.

## Quick Coleslaw (the acid that cuts the fat)
| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Bagged coleslaw mix | 14 oz |
| Mayonnaise (Duke's) | ½ cup |
| Apple cider vinegar | 2 tbsp |
| Dijon mustard | 1 tbsp |
| Sugar | 1 tbsp |
| Celery seed | ½ tsp |
| Kosher salt + black pepper | to taste |

**Method:** whisk the dressing, toss with the mix, chill 1 hr. This is the fresh, sharp bite that balances a rich plate of smoked meat — don't skip it.

## Southern Collard Greens
Low-and-slow greens with smoky pork. A BBQ-plate staple.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Collard greens, stemmed + chopped | 2 lb (2 bunches) |
| Thick-cut bacon or a smoked ham hock | 6 slices / 1 hock |
| Yellow onion, diced | 1 |
| Garlic, minced | 4 cloves |
| Low-sodium chicken broth | 4 cups |
| Apple cider vinegar | 2 tbsp |
| Brown sugar | 1 tbsp |
| Red pepper flakes | 1 tsp |
| Kosher salt + black pepper | to taste |

**Method:** render the **thick-cut bacon** (or brown the ham hock) in a Dutch oven, soften the **yellow onion + garlic**. Add greens, broth, vinegar, sugar, pepper flakes. Simmer **covered, low, 1.5–2 hrs** until silky. Serve with a splash of the "pot likker."

## Fried Okra
Crispy Southern classic. Fry inside, don't smoke.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Fresh okra, sliced ½" | 1 lb |
| Buttermilk | 1 cup |
| Yellow cornmeal | 1 cup |
| All-purpose flour | ½ cup |
| Kosher salt + black pepper | to taste |
| Peanut oil (for frying) | as needed |

**Method:** soak okra in **buttermilk** 10 min. Toss in **cornmeal + flour + salt + pepper**. Fry in **peanut oil at 350°F** in batches ~3–4 min until golden. Drain, salt right away.

## Hush Puppies
Fried cornmeal bites. Non-negotiable next to fish or pulled pork.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Yellow cornmeal | 1 cup |
| All-purpose flour | ½ cup |
| Baking powder | 1 tsp |
| Sugar | 1 tbsp |
| Yellow onion, grated | ¼ cup |
| Buttermilk | ¾ cup |
| Egg | 1 |
| Kosher salt | 1 tsp |
| Peanut oil (for frying) | as needed |

**Method:** mix dry, then stir in **grated yellow onion, buttermilk, egg**. Rest 10 min. Drop tablespoon scoops into **350°F peanut oil**, fry ~3 min until deep golden, turning. Drain.

## Smoked Corn on the Cob
Set it right on the grate — smoke loves corn.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Corn, husked | 6 ears |
| Salted butter, melted | 4 tbsp |
| Your BBQ rub | 1 tbsp |
| Cotija or parmesan (optional) | to finish |

**Method:** brush corn with **melted butter**, dust with rub. Smoke at **275–300°F ~45 min**, turning, until tender with a little char. Finish with more butter + cheese.

## Skillet Cornbread
Cast-iron cornbread with a crackly crust.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Yellow cornmeal | 1½ cups |
| All-purpose flour | 1 cup |
| Baking powder | 1 tbsp |
| Sugar | 2 tbsp |
| Buttermilk | 1½ cups |
| Eggs | 2 |
| Salted butter, melted | 6 tbsp |
| Kosher salt | 1 tsp |

**Method:** heat a **cast-iron skillet** with a pat of butter in a **425°F** oven. Mix dry, whisk in buttermilk + eggs + melted butter. Pour into the hot skillet (it should sizzle), bake **20–25 min** until golden. Jalapeño version: fold in 1 diced jalapeño + 1 cup shredded cheddar.

## Mustard Potato Salad
Tangy, yellow, picnic-style.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Yukon gold potatoes, cubed | 2 lb |
| Mayonnaise (Duke's) | ¾ cup |
| Yellow mustard | 3 tbsp |
| Dill pickle relish | 2 tbsp |
| Yellow onion, minced | ¼ cup |
| Eggs, hard-boiled + chopped | 3 |
| Apple cider vinegar | 1 tbsp |
| Paprika + kosher salt + black pepper | to taste |

**Method:** boil **Yukon golds** in salted water till fork-tender, drain, cool. Splash with vinegar while warm. Fold in everything else. Chill 1 hr+. Dust paprika on top.

## Texas Toast
The griddled garlic bread that soaks up sauce.

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Thick-cut white bread | 8 slices |
| Salted butter, softened | 6 tbsp |
| Garlic, minced | 3 cloves |
| Parmesan, grated | ¼ cup |
| Fresh parsley, chopped | 1 tbsp |

**Method:** mash **softened butter + garlic + parmesan + parsley**. Spread on both sides of the **thick-cut bread**. Griddle in a cast-iron pan over medium until golden both sides.

---

## Notes
- **Cast iron or foil pans** on the smoker — nothing you care about scrubbing.
- **Under the meat** = free flavor for beans and greens.
- Sides that take smoke well: beans, greens, mac, corn, potatoes. Delicate stuff (slaw) stays cold.
