# Cook Log — Publishing Idea (parked 🅿️)

_Someday/maybe. Revisit once the app + leftovers-playbook are further along. No action needed until Tom's ready._

## The idea
Put the Cook Log out for people — small side thing, "make a hundred bucks would be great."

## Honest read
- Recipe/cook-app market is **crowded** — a generic cook log won't sell.
- **The edge:** it's a file you *own* — offline, no account, no subscription, no bloat (BBQ/tinkerer crowd loves that), **+ the leftovers-playbook** ("what to do with the meat all week") is an uncommon hook. Lead with those, not "another cook app."

## The plan (in order)
1. **Free / pay-what-you-want** on **Gumroad** ($0 minimum) — kills the "will they pay" nerves, maximizes downloads, some people tip anyway.
2. Get a **handful of downloads + a couple ratings** = real validation.
3. **If** people like it → flip on a price (~$7) or lean into tips. ~15 sales = past $100.

## Why it's low-risk (Tom's worries, answered)
- **Cloned?** Nobody clones a hobby tool making pocket money; the edge is Tom's curation, not the HTML.
- **Hacked?** Static file — no server, no DB, no logins, Gumroad handles payment. Almost no attack surface.
- **Made fun of?** Can launch quietly under a handle; BBQ crowd respects homemade gear; worst case a stranger scrolls past.

## Smallest first test (when ready)
Polish a hair → upload to Gumroad free/PWYW with 3 screenshots → post in **one** BBQ subreddit/forum → see if a stranger bites. An afternoon, not a project.
