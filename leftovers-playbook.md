# Leftovers Playbook 🍖♻️

_What to do with leftover cooked meat — how to reheat it without wrecking it, and what to turn it into. One entry per meat. Add as you go._

---

## Ribeye (leftover cooked)

**Reheat without ruining it**
- **Cut it COLD** — dices/shreds far cleaner straight from the fridge (firm = clean cuts; warm = tears/squishes).
- **Revive gently:** warm butter + a splash of beef broth in a pan, drop the meat in just to warm through. **Don't over-simmer** — it's already cooked, you're reheating not cooking.

**Turn it into**
- **Philly cheesesteak** 🥩🧀 — diced (Tom's default) or shredded ribeye, onions + mushrooms (peppers optional), white cheese melted over, Kewpie mayo on untoasted Publix hoagie buns. Verdict: *"fantastic."* → full recipe: `cooks/2026-07-27-leftover-ribeye-philly-cheesesteak.md`

## Rotisserie Chicken (leftover)

**Reheat / prep without ruining it**
- **De-skin it if it's salty** — rotisserie skin holds a lot of salt; pulling it off tames the whole thing.
- For salads/cold use, just chop — no reheating needed.

**Turn it into**
- **Chicken salad** 🥗 — 3 cups chopped chicken (skin off) + 1 cup Kewpie mayo + 1/4 cup sour cream + 3 chopped celery sticks + red grapes. (Rich as written — dial mayo back if you want it lighter.) → full recipe: `cooks/2026-07-29-leftover-rotisserie-chicken-salad.md`
