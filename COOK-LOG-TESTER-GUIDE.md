# Cook Log — Private Trial

Thanks for helping test my BBQ Cook Log.

## Open it

**https://tomwood.dev/cook-log/**

No account is required. You do not need to install anything to try it.

## What it does

Cook Log helps you remember what worked on your grills: meat, fuel, wood, seasoning, temperatures, timing, results, and notes. You can also compare the same cook on two grills.

Your cooks and inventory stay privately in the browser on your device. They are not uploaded to a Cook Log account or shared database.

## Please try

1. Add a grill and a few inventory items.
2. Log one simple cook from beginning to end.
3. Close the page, reopen it, and confirm your cook is still there.
4. Search for the cook and edit one detail.
5. Open **Data** and export a backup.
6. Optional: tap **Install** and follow the instructions for your device.

## Private-trial note

Please do not store irreplaceable information yet. The app is in testing. Use **Data → Export backup** to protect anything you want to keep. Clearing the browser's site data or losing the device can erase local records.
